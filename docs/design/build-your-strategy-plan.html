Build your strategy: how we'll build it

Eight steps, in order. The first five are behind-the-scenes groundwork you won't see on screen. Step 6 is the pop-up itself, and step 7 connects it to the chain. Every step comes with its own automatic tests before the next one starts.

The steps

Groundwork (not visible) What you'll see Final check
  1. 1

    Talk to Kambala about margin and orders

    What gets builtTwo new requests: "how much margin does this basket need?" and "place this order".
    In demo modeA pretend version gives sample margins and fake order numbers, so nothing real is ever sent.
    Handles real orders: tested carefully
  2. 2

    Remember the strategy

    What gets builtA memory that holds the legs: Buy/Sell, lots, price, included or not. NIFTY and SENSEX each keep their own strategy.
    Rules it enforcesAt most 10 legs. One leg per contract. Each new leg starts as a Buy of 1 lot at the live price. Kept until you log out or close the tab.
    Groundwork
  3. 3

    Work out the margin

    What gets builtAsks Kambala for the margin 0.4 seconds after the last edit, and also for each leg on its own, to get the hedge benefit.
    Why it mattersFast typing doesn't flood Kambala, and an old answer can never replace a newer one.
    Groundwork
  4. 4

    Keep every leg's price live

    What gets builtA leg from another expiry keeps getting live prices, even when the chain is showing a different expiry.
    Why it mattersThe "Live" price and the net premium stay correct for calendar-style strategies.
    Groundwork
  5. 5

    Execute all, in a safe order

    What gets builtPlaces the buys first, then the sells, one at a time. If one leg is rejected, the rest are not sent.
    Result per legPlaced (with order number), Rejected (with Kambala's reason) or Not sent.
    Handles real orders: tested carefully
  6. 6

    The pop-up

    What you'll seeThe panel from the spec: one row per leg with B/S, expiry, strike, CE/PE, lots, price and Market; then margin, hedge benefit and net premium; then Lot ×, Intraday/Normal, Save to basket and Execute all.
    AlsoA "Place 3 orders" confirmation step inside the panel, the per-leg results, and Esc to fold it away. Works in light and dark.
    Visible
  7. 7

    Connect it to the chain

    What you'll seeA "Build your strategy" button at the bottom right with the number of legs. Ticking a strike adds a leg; deleting a leg unticks it.
    AlsoSave to basket hands the legs to TradeSmart's basket. Logging out clears the strategy. Add to Watchlist and Add to Basket still work as before.
    Visible
  8. 8

    Try it in the browser and write the hand-off notes

    What I doUse it end to end with demo data, in both themes and with the keyboard only, and save screenshots for you.
    For the tech teamA short note listing what they must connect: order and margin addresses, TradeSmart's basket, intraday permission, SENSEX market orders.
    Final check

Tricky cases we test on purpose

Same contract twiceChanging a leg to a strike already in the strategy is refused with "Already in your strategy".
Switching expiryA leg from the old expiry keeps its live price after you switch.
Slow margin answerAn old answer arriving late never replaces the newer figure.
Double click on "Place orders"Orders go out once; the panel locks while placing.
Damaged saved dataIf the remembered strategy is unreadable, the page starts with an empty one instead of breaking.

  • Catches mistakes early, which matters most for the steps that place orders.
