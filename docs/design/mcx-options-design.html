MCX options in the option chain

A client searches a commodity such as Crude Oil or Gold and opens its MCX option chain, with the same columns, analytics and strategy pop-up as NIFTY, using only the Kambala API.

Decisions

#Decision
M1Search finds all MCX commodities with options. No MCX quick buttons.
M2Each expiry is priced off the future it settles on (December options use the December future). The header price, ATM line, IV and Greeks change with the expiry.
M3Build your strategy: legs can be built, priced, margined and saved to basket. Execute all is off until Kambala confirms how MCX quantity is sent. One setting turns it on.
M4MCX market orders are off (they need a protection %), as on SENSEX.
M5Market hours 09:00–23:30 IST, 23:55 while the US is on daylight saving time. Already built: the feed dot follows them.

1. Search

Search also asks Kambala for MCX matches and shows each commodity once, after NSE and BSE results. A commodity with no options says so instead of opening an empty chain.

Search "CRU". A direct link works too: ?underlying=MCX:CRUDEOIL.

2. The chain follows the expiry's future

MCX options trade on futures, so there is no spot price. Each option expiry is matched to the future it settles on, and that future's price drives the chain.

CRUDEOILMCXDEC FUT5,412.00+64.00 (+1.20%)L 5,331 · H 5,429Lot 100
5d33d61dNext expiryExpiry 17 Dec 2026

Switch to the January expiry and the header shows the January future and its price.

SearchScrip · MCXFind the commodity and its nearest future.
GetLinkedScripsOption expiries and futures for the commodity.
GetUnderlyingExchTokenExact future for each expiry (fallback: first future on or after it).
GetOptionChain · MCXStrikes around the future's price, then live prices on the socket.
  • Columns and tabs stay the same. Lot size, tick and price precision come from each contract.
  • OI and volume are much smaller on MCX, so the "(In Lacs)" scaling will be checked against real numbers and may switch to plain numbers for MCX.
  • IV and Greeks use the same maths, with the future as the forward.

3. Build your strategy on MCX

Everything works except sending orders, until Kambala confirms whether MCX quantity is sent in lots or units. Getting this wrong could send a far larger order than intended.

  • Margin carries a "to be confirmed" note, since it uses the same quantity.

4. Demo mode

The simulated market gains CRUDEOIL, NATURALGAS, GOLD and SILVER with monthly futures and options, so the whole flow can be tried with ?feed=mock and no login.
