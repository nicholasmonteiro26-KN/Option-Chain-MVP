Build your strategy

A pop-up at the bottom right of the option chain that turns the ticked strikes into an editable strategy. The user sees the margin and premium, edits each leg in place, then saves it to a TradeSmart basket or places all the orders. Today, editing a basket means leaving the chain for the order book.

How legs behave

  • A new leg is a Buy of 1 lot, with a limit price set to the live price at the moment it's ticked.
  • Editing strike, expiry or CE/PE swaps the leg to that contract, and the ticks in the chain follow. The pickers offer only contracts that exist; other expiries are loaded the same way the chain loads them.
  • Live prices for every leg. A leg from an expiry the chain isn't showing stays subscribed, so its "Live" price keeps updating.
  • Quantity sent = lots × Lot× × the contract's own lot size (read per contract, never hard-coded). The row shows the quantity on hover, e.g. "2 lots × 65 = 130".
  • One strategy per underlying. Switching from NIFTY to SENSEX shows SENSEX's legs, and NIFTY's come back when the user returns. Legs are kept in this browser tab's session storage (per user), so they survive a refresh and are cleared on log out.
  • Up to 10 legs. An 11th tick shows "A strategy holds up to 10 legs" and doesn't tick. This keeps margin requests small.
  • Add to Watchlist / Add to Basket in the footer still work exactly as today, on the same ticked contracts.

Margin and premium

  • Margin needed: one GetBasketMargin call with every included leg (first leg at top level, the rest in basketlists). The figure shown is marginused.
  • Hedge benefit: the sum of each leg's margin on its own minus the combined margin. That's one extra call per leg, made only when the legs change, not on price ticks.
  • Net premium: Σ (sell price − buy price) × quantity, using each leg's limit price, or the live price for MKT legs. Worked out in the browser; no call needed.
  • When it's asked for: 400 ms after the last edit, with a newer request replacing an older one. While waiting, the figures show "…". If Kambala fails, they show "Margin unavailable" with a retry; Execute all stays usable, because Kambala's own order check still applies.
  • For margin, an MKT leg sends prctyp=MKT with prc set to the live price.

Execute all

Confirm

The pop-up turns into a confirmation step. It lists each included leg (side, contract, quantity, price or MKT), the margin needed and the product, then shows Place 3 orders and Back. This is not a browser dialog.

Place, buys first

Orders go one at a time with PlaceOrder: all buys, then all sells, in listed order within each group. Buying the hedge first lets the margin benefit apply to the short legs.

Stop on a rejection

If Kambala rejects a leg, the remaining legs aren't sent. Orders already placed stay placed; nothing is cancelled automatically.

  • While the market is closed, Execute all is off with the note "Market closed". After-market orders are out of scope for this version.
  • Demo mode (?feed=mock) never calls Kambala. A mock client returns a simulated margin and simulated order numbers.

How it's built

UnitJob
StrategyStoreSignal store for the legs of each underlying: add, remove, edit, include, Lot×, product, persistence. It is the single source for the chain's ticks.
OrdersClientbasketMargin(legs) and placeOrder(leg) over Kambala REST, plus a mock for demo mode.
StrategyMarginServiceDebounced margin and hedge-benefit requests, ignoring stale answers.
StrategyPanelComponentThe pop-up: leg rows, summary, action row, the confirmation step and per-leg results.
FooterActionsComponentGains the "Build your strategy (n)" button at the right.
Pagechecked is now read from StrategyStore. "Save to basket" is an INTEGRATION point: BasketService.add(legs), with side, qty, price and product per leg.

Tests

  • Store: tick and untick sync, edits that swap the contract, the 10-leg cap, per-underlying switching, session restore and clear on log out.
  • Margin: the request shape (top-level leg plus basketlists), debounce, stale answers ignored, hedge benefit, failure state.
  • Execute: buys before sells, stop after a rejection, per-leg status, off while the market is closed, mock never calls Kambala.
  • Panel: keyboard use (every control reachable, Esc folds the pop-up), the include box, Market switch, ↻ reset.

For the tech team to confirm

  • The exact PlaceOrder / GetBasketMargin paths on TradeSmart's v2 base. The doc lists /NorenWClientTP/…; the chain uses the v2 REST base.
  • Whether I (intraday) is in the account's allowed products (prarr) for NFO and BFO.
  • BFO market orders need mkt_protection. Until a value is agreed, the MKT switch is hidden on BFO (SENSEX) legs, which stay limit-only.
  • Orders larger than the exchange freeze quantity are rejected by Kambala and shown as Rejected. Splitting them is out of scope.

Out of scope

  • See in Builder (comes with the Strategy Builder), payoff chart, drag to reorder legs
  • After-market orders, stop-loss order types, splitting above the freeze quantity
  • Editing TradeSmart's saved baskets in place (that stays in the order book)
